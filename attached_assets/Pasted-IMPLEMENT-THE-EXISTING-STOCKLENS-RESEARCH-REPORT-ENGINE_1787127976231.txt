==================================================
FINANCIAL ANALYSIS
==================================================

Do NOT simply copy financial statements.

Calculate and interpret relevant metrics such as:

- revenue growth
- EBITDA growth
- EBITDA margin
- PAT growth
- EPS
- ROE
- ROCE
- ROIC where appropriate
- asset turnover where appropriate
- CFO
- CFO/PAT
- capex
- free cash flow where calculable
- debt
- net debt where calculable
- debt growth
- receivable days
- inventory days
- payable days
- working-capital days

Use deterministic calculations wherever possible.

Do not ask the AI to calculate simple arithmetic if code can calculate it.

AI should primarily interpret the calculated results.

==================================================
CONSOLIDATED VS STANDALONE — CRITICAL
==================================================

Never mix consolidated and standalone figures.

For every financial metric internally determine:

CONSOLIDATED
STANDALONE
NOT DETERMINED

Prefer consolidated information for group-level business analysis
where appropriate.

Use standalone where the analytical question specifically requires
the parent-company basis.

If both are available and materially different, do not silently
choose one.

If accounting basis cannot be established, do not guess.

The same rule applies to PEERS later.

Never compare a company's consolidated figure against another company's
standalone figure.

==================================================
INDUSTRY-SPECIFIC ANALYSIS
==================================================

Do not blindly apply every ratio to every company.

Determine the company's industry/business model and select relevant
metrics.

Examples:

Manufacturing / capital intensive:
- EV/EBITDA
- ROCE
- asset turnover
- margins
- capacity/utilisation where available

Banks/NBFCs/financial services:
- P/B
- ROE
- NIM
- GNPA
- NNPA
- capital adequacy where available

Asset-light / consumer / IT / pharma:
- P/E
- PEG where appropriate
- ROE/ROIC
- working capital
- cash conversion

Use genuine analyst judgement rather than mechanically applying a
checklist.

==================================================
MANAGEMENT ANALYSIS
==================================================

Separate:

FACT:
what management actually stated.

ANALYST INTERPRETATION:
what the statement could imply based on evidence.

Do not treat management guidance as guaranteed future performance.

Where historical guidance and actual results can be compared,
identify execution quality.
