REDESIGN THE USER-FACING EXPERIENCE AROUND THE CORE PRODUCT IDEA.

IMPORTANT:
The core product is NOT "AI equity research questions".
The core product is NOT a financial dashboard.
The core product is NOT a charting platform.

The product promise is:

"Type a stock name once. Get everything an investor needs to know about that company in one place, without reading the entire annual report or visiting 10 different websites."

The investor should be able to understand the important developments,
business, financials, industry, management, risks, shareholders,
valuation and other relevant information quickly.

The existing research questions/checklists are INTERNAL AI RESEARCH
INSTRUCTIONS. NEVER display those questions to the user.

DO NOT change:
- Supabase database
- research framework
- data ingestion architecture
- accounting-basis rules
- evidence system
- peer normalization
- API architecture

ONLY redesign the current user-facing UI/UX.

==================================================
1. HOMEPAGE
==================================================

Make the homepage extremely simple.

Hero:

"Research any stock in minutes."

Subheading:

"Stop reading hundreds of pages and searching across multiple
websites. Search a company and get the important information,
developments, risks and investor insights in one place."

Large search box:

"Search company name or ticker"

Example:
Reliance Industries / RELIANCE

Do not fill the homepage with:
- charts
- financial dashboards
- complicated filters
- technical indicators
- dozens of metrics

The primary action should be:
SEARCH COMPANY.

==================================================
2. COMPANY PAGE
==================================================

When the user searches a company, the first thing they should see
must be a highly readable SUMMARY.

Header:

COMPANY NAME
Ticker | Exchange | Sector | Industry

Then create:

"THE QUICK VIEW"

This is NOT a list of questions.

It should contain concise conclusions such as:

WHAT'S HAPPENING
A short explanation of the most important things currently
happening in the company.

WHY IT MATTERS
Explain why those developments matter to an investor.

BIGGEST POSITIVE
The most important positive development supported by evidence.

BIGGEST CONCERN
The most important concern supported by evidence.

WHAT TO WATCH
The 2–4 most important things investors should monitor.

INVESTOR TAKEAWAY
A concise synthesis of the company's current situation.

This section should feel like a very good equity analyst explaining
the company to another investor.

==================================================
3. FULL COMPANY RESEARCH
==================================================

Below the Quick View, show the deeper research in clear sections:

BUSINESS
Explain what the company actually does, its major businesses,
revenue/profit drivers and business strategy.

WHAT CHANGED
Show the important developments from recent quarters/periods.

FINANCIALS
Explain:
- revenue growth
- profitability
- margins
- EPS
- important changes
- what is driving the numbers

Do not dump financial statements on the user.

Explain what the numbers MEAN.

CASH FLOW & BALANCE SHEET
Explain:
- cash generation
- working capital
- debt
- leverage
- capex
- important balance-sheet changes

INDUSTRY
Explain:
- industry conditions
- industry cycle
- demand/supply
- relevant structural changes
- how these affect this specific company

MANAGEMENT
Explain:
- management strategy
- important guidance
- promises/targets
- whether previous guidance was delivered
- important changes in management commentary

SHAREHOLDERS
Explain:
- promoter holding changes
- pledging
- institutional ownership changes
- major shareholder developments
- dilution

GOVERNANCE & RELATED PARTIES
Only highlight things that are actually material.

PEERS
Explain where the company is stronger/weaker than relevant peers.

IMPORTANT:
Never compare incompatible standalone/consolidated figures.
Normalize the basis before presenting peer conclusions.

VALUATION
Explain the relevant valuation metrics for this company's industry
and what the current valuation appears to imply.

RISKS & RED FLAGS
Only show evidence-backed concerns.

Do NOT mechanically label something a red flag merely because a
checklist condition exists.

POSITIVE DEVELOPMENTS
Show meaningful improvements or positive developments supported by
evidence.

==================================================
4. USER EXPERIENCE
==================================================

The user should NOT feel like they are reading an annual report.

The experience should feel like:

"I searched Reliance and someone already did the research for me."

Use:
- short paragraphs
- clear headings
- bold important conclusions
- expandable deeper sections where appropriate
- simple language
- investor-friendly explanations

Avoid:
- huge tables
- unnecessary charts
- technical jargon without explanation
- repetitive information
- generic AI statements
- unnecessary disclaimers throughout the page
- showing internal research questions

The report can be long when the company genuinely requires it,
but every paragraph must add useful information.

==================================================
5. EVIDENCE
==================================================

Where the system has evidence available, allow the user to see the
source behind an important claim.

Example:

"Receivables increased significantly faster than revenue."

[Source: FY26 Annual Report]

Do not expose internal database structure or technical evidence
metadata to normal users.

The source should be accessible through a small "Source" or
"See source" interaction.

==================================================
6. ACCOUNTING BASIS
==================================================

The user-facing report must clearly distinguish:

CONSOLIDATED
STANDALONE

The AI should primarily explain the group-level economic picture
using consolidated information where appropriate, but it must select
the correct basis for each analytical question.

Never mix the two.

If a figure is standalone, clearly label it when material.

If consolidated and standalone figures materially differ, explain
why that difference matters.

==================================================
7. IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE
==================================================

The complexity should be BEHIND THE SCREEN.

The AI may internally analyze hundreds of questions/checks.

The investor should see only:

WHAT HAPPENED
WHY IT MATTERS
WHAT IS GOOD
WHAT IS BAD
WHAT TO WATCH
WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN
WHAT MANAGEMENT IS DOING
WHAT COULD GO WRONG
WHAT THE MARKET MAY BE EXPECTING
FINAL INVESTOR TAKEAWAY

Do not show the underlying checklist.
