We are now starting the architecture for the AI equity research engine.

Do NOT build the complete research engine yet.

Build only the foundation.

The product concept is:

A user searches for an Indian listed company and receives a concise,
investor-friendly research report. The AI must first understand the
company, its industry, business model and strategy, then decide what
analysis is relevant to that company.

The AI must NOT simply answer a fixed list of questions.

Create the following architecture:

1. COMPANY PROFILE
Store:
- company name
- ticker
- exchange
- sector
- industry
- sub-industry
- business description
- business segments
- revenue sources
- key products/services
- competitors

2. RESEARCH FRAMEWORK
Create a structure that can contain:
- business analysis
- industry analysis
- management analysis
- financial analysis
- cash flow analysis
- balance sheet analysis
- capital allocation
- shareholder/promoter analysis
- related-party transaction analysis
- accounting/red-flag analysis
- valuation analysis
- peer analysis
- industry-specific metrics
- recent developments/news
- investment risks
- positive developments

3. DYNAMIC ANALYSIS
The AI must determine which parts of the framework are relevant
depending on the company's industry and business model.

For example:
- Bank → NIM, NPA, CASA, credit growth, ROA, ROE, P/B
- EPC → order book, execution, working capital, receivables,
  margins, ROCE, debt
- Manufacturing → capacity utilization, asset turnover,
  margins, working capital, ROCE
- IT → revenue growth, margins, utilization, attrition,
  deal wins, cash generation

Do not apply irrelevant metrics.

4. EVIDENCE SYSTEM
Every important AI conclusion must be based on available source data.

Store:
- claim
- supporting source
- source date
- data point
- AI interpretation
- confidence level

The AI must distinguish:
FACT
INFERENCE
UNCERTAIN

If evidence is insufficient, the AI must say that it cannot determine
the conclusion rather than inventing an answer.

5. CROSS-CHECK ENGINE
Create the foundation for comparing:
- P&L vs balance sheet
- P&L vs cash flow
- revenue vs receivables
- profit vs operating cash flow
- debt vs interest expense
- capex vs depreciation
- management commentary vs actual results
- order book vs revenue
- promoter/shareholder changes vs company developments

6. FINAL REPORT STRUCTURE

The eventual user-facing report should NOT display questions.

It should display only useful conclusions:

- Business
- What is happening
- Financial picture
- Cash flow
- Balance sheet
- Business outlook
- Industry
- Management
- Shareholders
- Governance
- Related-party transactions
- Red flags
- Positive developments
- Valuation
- Investor takeaway

The report should be concise, factual, logical and investor-friendly.

It can be long when the company requires it, but every sentence must
provide useful information.

7. IMPORTANT AI RULES

The AI must:
- never fabricate financial information
- never invent reasons for a price movement
- never assume a red flag exists without evidence
- never treat every related-party transaction as suspicious
- never blindly trust management commentary
- never blindly trust one financial statement
- identify contradictions between sources
- clearly distinguish facts from interpretation
- say "insufficient evidence" when appropriate
- use the company's actual business model and industry context
- avoid generic financial commentary

For now, ONLY create the database/schema and backend architecture
required for this system.

Do not populate it with thousands of companies.
Do not build the final AI analysis yet.
