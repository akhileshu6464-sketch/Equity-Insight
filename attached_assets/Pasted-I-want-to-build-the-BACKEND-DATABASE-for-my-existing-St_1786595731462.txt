IMPORTANT:

Keep this extremely simple.

My website is NOT a stock screener.

The user simply searches for a stock and reads a simple, easy-to-understand research story.

"Type a stock. Understand the story in 5 minutes."

The website explains:

==================================================
STEP 5 — WRITING STYLE
==================================================

The future AI-generated research should follow this structure:

WHAT HAPPENED?

Explain the event.

WHY DID IT HAPPEN?

Explain the reason.

WHY DOES IT MATTER?

Explain the consequence.

IS IT A RED FLAG?

Explain whether investors should be concerned.

WHAT SHOULD WE WATCH NEXT?

Explain what could confirm or disprove the concern.

Use simple English.

Example:

Instead of:

"Working capital intensity increased materially."

Write:

"More money is getting stuck in the business because customers are taking longer to pay."

Instead of:

"Accrual quality deteriorated."

Write:

"Profit increased, but much less of that profit turned into actual cash."

The research should sound like a smart investor explaining the company to a friend.
